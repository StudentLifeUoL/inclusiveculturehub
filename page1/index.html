The Inequality Ladder

    You don’t choose where you start

    Experience how identity and geography affect life opportunities. You’ll be given a profile from a fictional world, then face four life stages.

    At each stage, reach for the path you’d want. Some paths will be closed to you, and you’ll see why. Seven other people, born on the same day as you, will climb alongside you.

    The people and places are fictional. The barriers are based on patterns found in many parts of the world.
