    Scenario

    You are a young group leader collaborating virtually with peers from the UK, Japan and Brazil. During a meeting, your Japanese teammate is expressive and enthusiastic, while your Brazilian colleague is quiet and reflective. You value fast-paced, direct conversation but wish to respect everyone’s style. How can you lead effectively?

    Pause and notice. Did either colleague’s behaviour surprise you? Cultural patterns describe tendencies across groups. They never tell you what an individual will do.
