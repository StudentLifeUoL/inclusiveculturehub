Cultivating Cultural Intelligence

A guided, multi-step reflection on leading diverse virtual teams. You’ll be able to download your responses at the end.

Welcome!

In this activity, you’ll reflect on how to lead a virtual team across cultures, focusing on core values, flexibility, and an action plan. At the end, you can download your personalised responses.

Scenario: You are a young group leader collaborating virtually with peers from the UK, Japan and Brazil. During a meeting, your Japanese teammate is expressive and enthusiastic, while your Brazilian colleague is quiet and reflective. You value fast-paced, direct conversation but wish to respect everyone’s style. How can you lead effectively?

Step 1: Core Values

Write up to five non-negotiable leadership values.
(Examples: Respect, Clarity, Inclusion, Efficiency)

Step 2: Flexibility

Note three ways you'll adapt your style.
(Examples: Slowing down the pace, allowing more silence, encouraging different forms of participation)

Step 3: Action Plan

Define what you'll start, stop and how to measure success.
