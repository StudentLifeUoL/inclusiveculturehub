🌍 Reflecting on the UN SDGs

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) aim to tackle the world's most urgent environmental, political, and economic challenges. In this reflective activity, we'd like you to consider:

"What would achieving the UN SDGs look like in your part of the world?"

📝 Instructions:

  • Take 5–10 minutes to reflect deeply on the changes you'd expect to see in your community or region.
  • Write your thoughts in the box below. Aim for 150–300 words.
  • Once done, you can download your response as a .txt file and optionally email it to your instructor for feedback.

Download and email your response to: sdg@london.ac.uk
