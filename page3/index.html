Reflecting on the UN SDGs

    Imagine it is 2030 and the goals have been met

    The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) aim to tackle the world’s most urgent environmental, political and economic challenges. In this reflective activity, we’d like you to consider:

    The question

    What would achieving the UN SDGs look like in your part of the world?
    • Take 5 to 10 minutes to reflect deeply on the changes you’d expect to see in your community or region.
    • Write your thoughts as a postcard from 2030. Aim for 150 to 300 words.
    • Download your response and, if you like, email it to your instructor for feedback.
    Your town, city or region. Optional.

    Which goals matter most where you live?

    Choose up to three. They will appear on the stamp of your postcard, so you can keep them in mind as you write.

    Need a nudge?
    • What has changed in your community by 2030?
    • Who benefits, and who might still be left behind?
    • What did people, organisations or government do to get there?
    • What part did you play?
